# Scoring Methodology

RentRelief produces a transparent 0–100 screening score using weighted local signals:
- rent burden: 31%
- utility burden: 12%
- commute burden: 18%
- income trend pressure: 14%
- rent growth: 10%
- total housing + commute burden pressure: 15%

Bands:
- 0–24.9 Low
- 25–49.9 Moderate
- 50–74.9 High
- 75–100 Critical

The score is directional screening infrastructure, not a validated measure of individual financial hardship.
